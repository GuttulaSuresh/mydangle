MYDANGLE — QUICK START

1. Open MyDangle.xcodeproj in Xcode.
2. If Xcode requests signing, select your Apple Development Team under Signing & Capabilities.
3. Press Command + R.
4. Look for the MyDangle icon in your Mac menu bar.
5. Control + Option + D = show/hide.
6. Control + Option + K = push/swing.
7. Drag the charm to flick it. Grab the tiny top anchor dot to move it horizontally.

Everything runs locally. No server or account is required.
