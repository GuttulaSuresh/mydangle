MYDANGLE V2

1. Open MyDangleV2.xcodeproj in Xcode.
2. Select My Mac.
3. If Xcode asks for signing, choose your Apple Development Team.
4. Press Command + R.
5. Wait about one second.
6. Look for "🧿 MyDangle" in the menu bar.
7. The hanging charm should appear from the top center.

This is a fresh project. Do not merge it with the old MyDangle folder.
