MYDANGLE 1.2 — DOUBLE-CLICK FIX

1. Unzip.
2. Double-click Install MyDangle.command.
3. After installation, MyDangle.app is in Home > Applications.
4. Double-clicking MyDangle.app now opens a visible control window and shows the hanging charm.
5. Closing the control window does not quit the charm; use the MyDangle menu or Command-Q to quit.
